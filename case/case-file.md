## Where it stands now
- **Verdict (29 Sep 2026):** an independent commission found City breached Premier League rules on 114 of the 115 charges. City were found to have breached every financial charge. The one charge not proven was among the non-cooperation charges. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/9/29/man-city-guilty-of-all-serious-financial-breach-charges-premier-league), [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13290810/man-city-charges-verdict-club-found-guilty-of-all-charges-related-to-serious-breaches-of-premier-league-financial-rules)

  - *Unconfirmed:* which non-cooperation charge was not proven.
- **Sanction:** NOT decided yet. The same commission sets it at a separate hearing. The range runs from a fine to a points deduction to expulsion. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/29/sport/premier-league-outlines-manchester-city-rules-breaches)
  - *Single source:* the sanction is expected "by the end of January" 2027. [CaughtOffside](https://www.caughtoffside.com/2026/10/01/manchester-city-punishment-decided-by-january/)
- **Appeal:** City say they will appeal. The deadline is **Fri 2 Oct 2026**. [Inside World Football](https://www.insideworldfootball.com/2026/10/01/manchester-city-have-friday-deadline-to-initiate-appeal-against-epl-charges/)

  - No appeal hearing date has been set.

[Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13594069/man-city-charges-how-premier-league-club-plan-to-argue-their-innocence-in-appeal), [Irish News](https://www.irishnews.com/sport/soccer/man-city-to-claim-sponsor-money-was-paid-by-abu-dhabi-government-during-appeal-ZLHPIYFRH5I7ZBKKJMB22MILZM/)

## What the commission found (plain English)
- **Disguised owner funding:** City booked about £949.9m of sponsorship from Abu Dhabi partners.
  - The commission found £830.69m of it was funded by the owner (ADUG), not the sponsors. It called the arrangement a "Disguised Funding Scheme" and called the contracts "shams".
  - The partners named are Etihad, Aabar, the Abu Dhabi Tourism Authority and Etisalat.
  - The period is 2009-10 to 2017-18.
  - Sources: [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13594069/man-city-charges-how-premier-league-club-plan-to-argue-their-innocence-in-appeal), [readmancity](https://readmancity.com/2026/09/30/man-city-financial-ruling-commission-findings-sponsorship/)
- **The PL's headline figure:** revenue was inflated and costs reduced by "more than £900m" in total. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/9/29/man-city-guilty-of-all-serious-financial-breach-charges-premier-league)
- **Mancini side contract:** Roberto Mancini had a deal with Al Jazira (Abu Dhabi) worth £1.75m a year for about four days' work. [readmancity](https://readmancity.com/2026/09/30/man-city-financial-ruling-commission-findings-sponsorship/)
- **Fordham (image rights):** the commission found Fordham was "effectively a front for ADUG" and that payments kept player costs off City's books. [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13593407/man-city-charges-verdict-key-findings-the-fordham-arrangement-effectively-a-front-for-abu-dhabi-united-group)
- **The original 115 charges:**
  - 54: inaccurate financial information (2009-18)
  - 14: inaccurate pay details for players and the manager
  - 5: UEFA FFP breaches
  - 7: PL profit and sustainability breaches (2015-18)
  - 35: failure to cooperate (2018-23)
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_City_F.C.%E2%80%94Premier_League_financial_breaches_case)

## How appeals work (PL Rule W)
- The appeal goes to a new three-member Appeal Board, chaired by someone who has held judicial office.
- The hearing is meant to be short (about 5 days), with a decision expected within weeks rather than years.
- *Unclear:* whether the sanction is set before or after the appeal.
- Sources: [Sky Sports explainer](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13593377/man-city-premier-league-charges-appeal-process-punishment-hearing-and-what-happens-now-after-guilty-verdict), [readmancity](https://readmancity.com/2026/09/26/man-city-premier-league-charges-appeals-process-explained/)

## Precedents for punishment
- **Everton:** 10 points, reduced to 6 on appeal, then 2 more (2023-24).
- **Nottingham Forest:** 4 points.
- **Chelsea:** £10.75m fine after self-reporting.
