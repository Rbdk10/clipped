# Day 4: City's sponsor turns on the Premier League

_2026-10-02 · 68s video_

## Today's update

Man City's biggest sponsor says it's considering legal action against the Premier League. And the fallout could reach a long way beyond football. Quick recap. On 29 September, an independent commission found City broke Premier League rules on 114 of the 115 charges. It found sponsorship deals were used to inflate City's revenue and cut costs by more than £900m. City deny any wrongdoing. They're appealing, and the punishment still hasn't been decided. Now Etihad, the Abu Dhabi airline on City's shirt, has hit back. In a statement, it categorically rejects any suggestion it was involved in improper commercial arrangements. Etihad says the Premier League never contacted it during the investigation, and it's now taking legal advice. But the bigger worry might be in Westminster. Last year, Bloomberg reported the UAE had raised the case with the UK government. UK officials were privately worried the verdict could hurt Abu Dhabi's investment in Britain. To be clear, nobody has publicly threatened to pull investment. The government said it's the Premier League's decision. So should a sponsor be able to take on the league? Yes or no?
