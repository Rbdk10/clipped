## Today's update

Today is the deadline for Manchester City to appeal the biggest verdict in Premier League history. But the bigger story might be what comes after it. Quick recap. On Tuesday, an independent commission found City broke Premier League rules on 114 of the 115 charges. City deny any wrongdoing. They say they will appeal, and that the ruling contains clear errors. Now, the punishment. The Times reports the commission will hold a separate hearing to decide City's sanction, and it could come within weeks, not months.
